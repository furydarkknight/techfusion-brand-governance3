Detailed Breakdown of Delivered Improvements

1. Multi-Modal Computer Vision Brand Inspector

Audits marketing banners and graphic assets. Uses Pillow-based quantization to extract dominant swatches, mathematically calculates WCAG 2.1 AA luminance contrast ratio (4.5:1 requirement), detects prohibited legacy colors (#EF4444), and enforces 24px logo safe zones.

2. Vector-Grounded Semantic RAG & Dynamic Ingestion

Dynamic ingestion pipeline for brand books, SEC 10-K filings, and ESG standards. Chunks unstructured text, generates TF-IDF and BM25 token vectors, and executes sub-millisecond cosine similarity search to ground audit findings in audited evidence with percentage confidence scores.

3. In-Context CMS & Browser Integration Suite

Realistic mock interfaces for Google Docs, Figma Canvas, and WordPress. Writers type naturally while floating guardrail pills appear with sub-15ms response times, offering 1-click surgical corrections and pre-publish validation gates.

Responsible AI, Security, Limitations & Roadmap

Responsible AI & Privacy

• Zero Leakage: Analysis executes locally; confidential pre-release materials are never dispatched to external third-party LLM endpoints.
• Explainability by Design: Flags map to concrete rule IDs (`GL-CLAIM-01`, `GL-VIS-01`) and source documents (`SEC-Q4-Metrics.pdf`).
• Human Oversight: Human reviewers retain ultimate authority; governance overrides mandate an immutable business justification.

Limitations & 2027 Roadmap

• Current Limitation: Video inspection runs on extracted keyframes rather than live video streaming.
• Q1 2027: Official Google Workspace and Figma Marketplace extension releases.
• Q2 2027: Multi-language localization guardrails for Japanese, German, and French enterprise copy.
• Q3 2027: Video cadence and motion logo safe-zone tracking for YouTube & TikTok ads.
